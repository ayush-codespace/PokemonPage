Squritle Information

Squirtle is a small, water-type Tiny Turtle Pokémon introduced in Generation 1

Squirtle is one of the most iconic and beloved Water-type Pokémon, first introduced in Generation I as a starter option in the Kanto region. Resembling a small, light-blue turtle, it is instantly recognizable by its large brown shell, which it uses not only for defense but also to reduce water resistance for high-speed swimming. Squirtle is famous for its playful yet mischievous personality, perfectly captured in the anime by the legendary "Squirtle Squad"—a group of rogue Squirtle who wore sunglasses before joining forces with Ash Ketchum. In battle, it sprays powerful streams of water or retreats into its shell to execute moves like Withdraw and Rapid Spin. As it gains experience, Squirtle evolves into the powerful Wartortle and eventually into the formidable Blastoise, cementing its legacy as a fan-favorite cornerstone of the Pokémon franchise.
