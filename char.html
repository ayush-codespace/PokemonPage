Charmander Information

Charmander is an iconic Fire-type Lizard Pokémon introduced in Generation I as one of the three original Kanto starter options.

Charmanderis a small, bipedal Pokémon that mirrors the appearance of an orange, reptilian dinosaur or salamander, characterized by its cream-colored underbelly and large, expressive blue eyes. Its most defining feature is the vibrant, flickering flame burning at the tip of its slender tail, a biological indicator that has blazed continuously since its birth. This flame acts as a direct barometer of Charmander's health and emotional state, burning vigorously when it is full of life, growing into a roaring blaze when enraged, and dwindling precariously to a faint glimmer if it falls ill or becomes weak. Renowned for its passionate, fierce, yet deeply affectionate nature, this legendary Fire-type starter holds the prestigious #0004 spot in the National Pokédex , continuing to capture the hearts of trainers globally as one of the ultimate icons of the Pokémon universe.
